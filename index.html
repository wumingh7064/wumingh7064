Privacy Policy

ROUTE  ·  Last updated: [FILL IN: e.g. 2 September 2026]

In short: ROUTE does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the game remembers — your level progress, coins and hints — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking.

1. Who we are

ROUTE ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game published on Google Play under the package name com.wmh.arrow.animal. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

2. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for, access, or transmit any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Location of any kind
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Any information that identifies you personally

There is no account system. You cannot sign in, and we have no way to associate the game with a person.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Which level you have reached
  • Your coin balance and remaining hints
  • A snapshot of a level you left part-way through

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data from your device settings.

4. Advertising

The game shows a small banner at the bottom of the screen promoting other titles. This banner is marked with an "AD" label so it is always identifiable as advertising.

These promotions are our own, and the images and text for them are included in the app itself. The current version contains no third-party advertising SDK, does not read your advertising identifier, and does not build a profile of you or track you across apps or websites. The ads you see are the same for everyone — they are not personalised and are not based on anything about you.

5. Analytics

The current version does not send any analytics or usage data. Engine-level analytics services are disabled in this build.

6. Permissions

The game declares the standard INTERNET permission, which the Unity game engine includes by default. The current version does not make network requests and works entirely offline.

7. Children

ROUTE is a general-audience puzzle game. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export, correct, or delete on your behalf. All game data lives on your device and is under your control: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game begins collecting data — for example if online features or a third-party advertising network are added — we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back after updates.
